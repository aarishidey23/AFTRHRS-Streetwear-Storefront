## Brand & Style

This design system channels an uncompromising intersection of luxury editorial layout and underground rave/streetwear aesthetics. Built for digital-native tastemakers and subcultural fashion consumers, the UI balances ruthless functional brutalism with tactile editorial finesse.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Raw & Unfiltered:** High-contrast graphic borders, industrial data markers, and exposed structural grids establish a visceral, unfiltered fashion-house atmosphere.
- **Electric Exclusivity:** Acid neon focal points pierce deep void surfaces, evoking late-night warehouse drops and high-heat retail exclusivity.
- **Editorial Poise:** Monumental type scales, tight tracking, and magazine-spread composition juxtapose spontaneous micro-labels ("LOWKEY ICONIC", "DROP 004").

### Visual Grammar
- **Base Canvas:** Obsidian black void foundation layered with hairline zinc gridding.
- **Accents:** Electric Acid Lime (`#D4FF00`) and Cyber Cherry (`#FF2A5F`) deployed strictly for high-priority purchase intent, scarcity indicators, and critical metadata callouts.
- **Surface Texture:** Solid zinc panels, brutalist rectangular containers, and precise architectural division lines—eschewing blurred glass in favor of razor-sharp spatial definitions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual friction: deep, light-absorbing darks disrupted by high-voltage chromatic spikes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#D4FF00`):** Electric Acid Lime. Reserved for dominant calls-to-action, release alerts, cursor states, and key navigational highlights. Never use for long-form reading.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF2A5F`):** Cyber Cherry Red. Indicates drop countdowns, limited stock warnings, active wishlist hearts, and editorial collection labels.
- **Tertiary Neutral (`#E4E4E7`):** Off-White Zinc. Primary typography, high-visibility pricing (₹), and crisp iconography.
- **Supporting Greys:**
  - `#A1A1AA`: Secondary product descriptions, collection metadata, editorial captions.
  - `#71717A`: Inactive options, breadcrumbs, sold-out sizes, and micro-grid rules.
- **Surface & Void Infrastructure:**
  - `#0A0A0A`: Infinite background canvas (Void Black).
  - `#121212`: Baseline container background for product listing matrices.
  - `#18181B`: Elevated container fills, drawer panels, modal surfaces.
  - `#27272A`: Hairline partition border for editorial separation.

## Typography

Typography governs the tension between raw industrial utility and heavy editorial visual weight.

### Typography Application Rules
- **Headline Architecture (`Syne`):** Always render display headings and hero banners in full uppercase with tight line heights. Kerning must be clamped tightly (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to mirror luxury fashion prints.
- **Narrative Content (`Inter`):** Neutral, hyper-legible neutral sans applied across product copy, garment descriptions, checkout flows, and review logs.
- **Technical & Editorial Badging (`Space Mono`):** Used exclusively for system states, prices, SKU designations, inventory counters (`ONLY 3 REMAINING`), drop dates, and sticker badges. Always force uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- **Currency Integration:** Render the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) directly in `Space Mono` or semibold `Inter` immediately adjacent to numeric values without an empty space (e.g., `₹4,999`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a fluid, architectural 12-column grid system bounded by strict geometric containment.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin `1rem` (`16px`), gutter `1rem` (`16px`). Product grids default to 2 columns to emulate endless social feed boards. Full-width ticker marquees extend edge-to-edge ignoring outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout. Margin `2rem` (`32px`), gutter `1.25rem` (`20px`). Product grids display in 2 or 3 columns with alternating height ratios.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. Margin `3rem` (`48px`), gutter `1.5rem` (`24px`). Editorial lookbook spreads can span uneven split-column formats (e.g., 7 columns for primary media, 5 columns for product details).

### Structural Rhythms
- Modules are bounded by explicit structural wireframe rules (`1px solid #27272A`).
- Visual rhythm alternates between high-density technical modules (tight padding, high content compression) and cavernous, oversized negative space in editorial hero sections.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects ambient blurry drop-shadows and skeuomorphic light sources. Elevation is communicated through stark planar contrast, strict tonal layering, and razor-thin architectural boundaries.

### Depth Mechanisms
- **Base Planar System:**
  - `Canvas`: #0A0A0A (absolute bottom layer).
  - `Sub-Surface`: #121212 (nested cards, gallery tiles).
  - `Raised Surface`: #18181B (drawers, sliding bag panels, sticky headers).
  - `Floater Panels`: #242427 (filter flyouts, context menus).
- **Boundary Precision:** Surfaces do not dissolve into shadows. Every elevated panel, modal, or dropdown uses a crisp `1px solid #27272A` perimeter. On interactive focus or active trigger, transition borders instantaneously to `#D4FF00` or `#E4E4E7`.
- **Hard Offsets (Brutalist Stacks):** When absolute focus is required (e.g., promotional popups or sticker badges), use a zero-blur, high-contrast offset border shadow: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #D4FF00`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is completely non-rounded (`roundedness: 0`), reinforcing brutalist, underground print aesthetics.

### Geometric Execution
- **Strict Rectilinear Surfaces:** Product cards, dialogs, image wrappers, inputs, and flyout navigation panels possess pure 90-degree squared corners (`border-radius: 0px`).
- **Pill Exception for Micro-Stickers:** Badges and select tag buttons may use full rounded capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`) purely as an intentional contrast mechanism to mimic physical vinyl stickers slapped onto cardboard boxes and brutalist architecture.
- **Image Ratios:** Media containers adhere strictly to editorial ratios: `3:4` for portrait product crops, `1:1` for detail swatches, and `16:9` for cinematic campaign lookbooks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric Inversion):**
  - Background: `#D4FF00`. Text: `#0A0A0A` (`Space Mono`, 12px, bold, uppercase).
  - Border: None. Radius: `0px`.
  - Hover: Invert immediately to background `#FFFFFF` with `#0A0A0A` text, or invert to `#0A0A0A` with `1px solid #D4FF00` and `#D4FF00` text.
- **Secondary Action (Brutalist Wire):**
  - Background: Transparent. Text: `#E4E4E7`.
  - Border: `1px solid #27272A`. Radius: `0px`.
  - Hover: Border color `#D4FF00`, text color `#D4FF00`.
- **Quick-Add Button:**
  - Placed directly inside product cards, appearing on hover or fixed at card bottom on mobile. Text: `+ QUICK ADD`.

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- **Visual Composition:** Solid `#121212` container encased in `1px solid #27272A`.
- **Image Layer:** 3:4 aspect ratio. Hover triggers an instant swap or smooth cut between editorial model shot and raw flat-lay detail shot.
- **Wishlist Trigger:** Top-right corner icon button. Borderless, featuring an outlined heart that fills with `#FF2A5F` on active state.
- **Sticker Overlays:** Top-left corner floating tag (e.g., `THE DROP`, `FEW LEFT`) in `#0A0A0A` on `#D4FF00` or `#FF2A5F`.
- **Data Footer:** Price (`₹X,XXX`) rendered in `Space Mono` alongside compact garment title (`Inter`, 14px).

### Badges & Sticker Tags
- High-contrast micro-labels using `Space Mono`, size `10px`, all-caps.
- Two variants:
  - *Box Tag:* Squared 0px box, `1px solid #27272A`, black background, white text.
  - *Vinyl Sticker:* Full pill shape, solid `#D4FF00` background with `#0A0A0A` text, rotated `-2deg` for controlled graphic anarchy.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#121212`, border `1px solid #27272A`, text `#E4E4E7`. Placeholder in `#71717A`. On focus: `1px solid #D4FF00` with zero outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square boxes (`14px x 14px`), border `1px solid #3F3F46`. Checked state: solid fill `#D4FF00` with `#0A0A0A` center icon.

### Marquee Tickers
- Infinite CSS-scrolling horizontal band. Background `#D4FF00` with `#0A0A0A` text, or `#121212` with `1px solid #27272A` borders and `#E4E4E7` text.
- Typeset in bold `Space Mono` or `Syne`, featuring repeating brand mantras, drop countdowns, and shipping announcements divided by asterisks or lightning marks.

### Drop / Countdown Timer
- Fixed or banner-level countdown display with segmented digit boxes (`#18181B`), each bordered in `#27272A`.
- Numerals rendered in `Space Mono` with glowing `#FF2A5F` accents for scarcity indication.
